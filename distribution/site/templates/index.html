Why now

Payments are going production. Trust isn't.

An agent can read a 402 challenge, sign a payment, and retry in seconds. That speed is useful — and it makes the next question unavoidable.

Agents pay strangers

Unattended transactions have no cashier, no chargeback desk, and no one to ask whether the counterparty is worth paying.

A settlement is evidence

A settled payment proves that money moved. It does not prove delivery, and it says nothing about who was on the other side.

Unknown is not bad

A new seller has no record. Treating missing evidence as a low score punishes the honest newcomer and rewards the wrong signal.

One product, many partners

How it works

A check in the path, not a judge on top.

The useful place for a reputation signal is before the signature, where it can change a decision and be recorded.

Payment request

The agent receives a 402 challenge or a sats invoice with a payee, an amount, and a network.

Check

It asks Zapproof what settled-payment evidence exists for that payee, with the rules and the limits attached.

Pay through any rail

It pays in sats through a POS or wallet, or in USDC over x402, and the settlement becomes evidence for the next check — not a promise about this one.
